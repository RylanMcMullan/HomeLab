# Establish NAS Storage

**Status:** Planned; no dedicated NAS, SAN, external array, or other network storage is deployed.

**Related plans:** [Deploy Jellyfin](deploy-jellyfin.md), [Deploy Nextcloud](deploy-nextcloud.md), and [Deploy Bitwarden](deploy-bitwarden.md) depend on the storage and recovery decision. [Segment HomeLab Network](segment-homelab-network.md) governs any NAS network placement.

## Candidate review — 2026-10-01

The intended purchase is a diskless NAS enclosure near or under $300, with up to roughly $600 available for the initial enclosure, SSD, and HDD together. The proposed initial layout is one M.2 NVMe SSD for application/VM storage and one 4 TB or larger NAS-rated CMR HDD as a Basic bulk-data pool. A second compatible HDD would later convert that pool to RAID 1 after an independent backup and restore check. Do not combine the SSD and HDD in one RAID pool or use RAID 0 for data that needs protection. A 500 GB SSD is the provisional starting size; 256 GB is plausible only for a measured, small container set without VM images or substantial databases. Size the SSD again after the final service placement and retention policies are known. [UGREEN confirms](https://ai.ugreen.com/blogs/how-to/mix-ssds-hdds-nas-setup) that the DXP2800 M.2 slots can form a separate SSD pool rather than only a cache; check the exact drive models against its [compatibility list](https://ai.ugreen.com/pages/compatibility).

The [UGREEN NASync DXP2800](https://ai.ugreen.com/products/ugreen-nasync-dxp2800-refurbished) is the leading hardware candidate, not a selected or deployed device. Its Intel N100, 8 GB RAM (manufacturer maximum 16 GB), two SATA bays, two M.2 slots, and one 2.5 GbE port allow the proposed physical drive layout. UGREEN documents Docker and VM support for DXP models, and [Basic-to-RAID-1 conversion](https://support.ugnas.com/detail/article/en-US/305) on supported UGOS Pro versions. The public US refurbished page displayed $269.99 for L1 and a "Sold out" label on 2026-10-01; it also offered a three-month warranty and quality-issue-only returns. On the same date, the operator reported reaching UGREEN checkout for the device without a stock warning. The page and checkout observations conflict; successful order availability remains unverified. Use the live checkout total and actual order acceptance when purchasing.

The current NAS-hosting proposal uses one switch access VLAN for the NAS and its direct applications, with separate Docker networks, storage permissions, application authentication, and narrow host/firewall rules. This is a conscious shared-host boundary: the router VM cannot separate NAS applications from one another on that VLAN, and the NAS administrator has access to their underlying storage. Keep UGOS Pro, SSH, Docker management, and storage protocols private; only reviewed application origins may receive a Cloudflare route. [UGREEN documents virtual bridging](https://support.ugnas.com/detail/article/en-US/387) for VMs, but the reviewed vendor material does not establish per-guest tagged VLAN assignment on the DXP2800. [UGOS Pro supports administrator SSH/root access](https://support.ugnas.com/detail/article/en-US/481); that permits investigation, but custom Linux VLAN commands are not documented as a persistent supported configuration and are not a purchase assumption. If a service later requires a distinct VLAN, prove model- and firmware-specific behavior or move it to an isolated Proxmox guest using narrowly permissioned NAS storage. Homepage and Uptime Kuma can provide one navigation/status view but do not manage NAS guests as Proxmox guests; NAS VM/container administration remains in the NAS console or an additional management tool.

The [TerraMaster F2-424](https://www.terra-master.com/products/f2-424) has two SATA bays, two M.2 slots, 8 GB RAM expandable to 32 GB, and two 2.5 GbE ports, but the official US listing was $379.99 and out of stock on 2026-10-01. [TOS 7 documents a VLAN ID for VM virtual networks](https://help.terra-master.com/docs/TOS7/application/vms/), making it the clearer supported option if separate VM VLANs become mandatory. The cheaper [F2-425](https://www.terra-master.com/en-gb/products/f2-425) has no M.2 slots, so an SSD would occupy one of its two HDD bays and prevent the proposed two-HDD mirror. The [F2-425 Plus](https://www.terra-master.com/products/f2-425-plus) offers three M.2 slots, 8 GB RAM expandable to 32 GB, and two 5 GbE ports, but its official US listing showed $424.99 and out of stock. The DXP2800 remains the stronger fit for the stated starting budget if its checkout and application tests succeed. Recheck alternatives and stock near the actual purchase date.

**2026-10-01 listing follow-up:** The operator confirmed the intended UGREEN checkout selection is **L1**, accepting its cosmetic wear grade. [UGREEN lists L1 as 80–95% new with signs of wear, a three-month warranty, and 30-day quality-issue-only returns](https://ai.ugreen.com/products/ugreen-nasync-dxp2800-refurbished); cosmetic grade alone does not establish hardware health. The operator also reported an [Amazon F2-425 listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FHVZ53RN) in stock near $250 (reduced from about $300). Amazon listing contents and live checkout price could not be fetched independently. [TerraMaster's F2-425 specification](https://www.terra-master.com/products/f2-425) lists 4 GB DDR4 in one replaceable slot, a 16 GB maximum, two SATA bays, one 2.5 GbE port, Docker support, and no M.2 slot. Its product headline says N95 while the detailed specification says N5095; confirm the exact CPU/SKU with the seller before purchase. Four GB is a likely memory constraint for concurrent Nextcloud AIO, Jellyfin, Bitwarden, NAS services, and especially any VM: [Nextcloud AIO's guidance](https://github.com/nextcloud/all-in-one/discussions/1335) calls for at least 2 GB when optional components run, and [TerraMaster's VM guidance](https://help.terra-master.com/docs/TOS7/application/vms/) recommends 4 GB or more for VMs. A RAM replacement would add cost but would not solve the missing internal SSD slot. With two mirrored HDDs, application/database workloads would live on HDD unless an external SSD is adopted and its storage-pool/application behavior verified. For this storage layout, the F2-425's lower enclosure price does not outweigh those compromises.

**L1 acceptance after delivery:** The operator accepts cosmetic wear and plans to test every port. During the 30-day quality-issue return period, inspect the enclosure, drive trays, connectors, and power supply; verify boot, updates, stable Ethernet, each USB port, HDMI, both SATA bays, and both M.2 slots where test media is available. Check that the fan runs, temperatures remain reasonable under sustained disk/network activity, and the NAS restarts cleanly. Test bays and slots with disposable data before storing the only copy of anything; do not remove a live drive to test another bay. Record any failed connector, unstable operation, or abnormal cooling promptly for a quality-issue return. The disks will be purchased separately, so their health and warranty should be assessed independently of enclosure cosmetics.

The DXP2800's four-core CPU and 8 GB starting RAM make it a small secondary application host, not a second equivalent Proxmox node. Prefer Jellyfin, a limited Nextcloud AIO stack, and official Bitwarden Lite as Docker workloads before adding general-purpose VMs. Jellyfin's [hardware guidance](https://jellyfin.org/docs/general/administration/hardware-selection/) lists the N100 with 8 GB system RAM as suitable for an integrated-graphics server; verify GPU access and transcoding on UGOS Pro. Nextcloud AIO is a [multi-container stack](https://github.com/nextcloud/all-in-one), and its Docker-socket, storage-path, update, and backup behavior must be proved on this NAS. [Bitwarden Lite](https://bitwarden.com/help/install-and-deploy-lite/) is a small single-container candidate; verify the installed Docker Engine version, database choice, and full client behavior. If concurrent use, optional Nextcloud components, or VMs cause pressure, measure and consider RAM expansion before assigning more services. The [existing switch](../network/topology.md#tp-link-tl-sg108pe-managed-switch) has unverified negotiated link speeds; a 2.5 GbE NAS port does not establish a 2.5 GbE end-to-end path.

Cloudflare costs are a separate operating decision. [Cloudflare Registrar](https://developers.cloudflare.com/registrar/) offers at-cost registration and renewal, with the exact domain/TLD price to be checked before purchase. A paid [Cloudflare Pro or Business zone](https://www.cloudflare.com/plans/) adds web features but does **not** waive the [public Tunnel video/large-file restriction](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/delivering-videos-with-cloudflare/). [Cloudflare Stream](https://developers.cloudflare.com/stream/pricing/) charges for stored and delivered video minutes and is a separate hosted media pipeline, not a documented drop-in route for a local Jellyfin library and its native clients. An ordinary public Tunnel therefore remains unsuitable as the assumed Jellyfin media path. Keep the Jellyfin public route unresolved until a permitted delivery method that works with the intended clients and MFA is identified; test large-file Nextcloud behavior separately.

## Purpose and dependencies

The existing [HP EliteDesk](../hosts/hp-elitedesk.md) uses internal-only storage at last verification. Its exact installed storage specification belongs in the host record. A larger, durable storage foundation is required before data-heavy or recovery-critical services are treated as production-ready.

Planned consumers include:

- [Convert Acer to AI Node](convert-acer-to-ai-node.md): model files, datasets, knowledge bases, and selected migration data.
- [Deploy Jellyfin](deploy-jellyfin.md): media libraries and growth capacity.
- [Deploy Nextcloud](deploy-nextcloud.md): primary user data and database/application recovery.
- [Deploy Bitwarden](deploy-bitwarden.md): possible NAS-hosted application plus encrypted backups and tested recovery artifacts, not an unprotected vault export.
- Proxmox guests: backups with defined retention and restore tests.

Before the NAS is bought, available Raspberry Pi USB-SSD capacity is a proposed interim Minecraft backup destination. Confirm free capacity, backup consistency, retention, and a restore. A copy on the same SSD is useful for rollback but does not survive SSD failure. The NAS is a candidate for service applications, data, and local backups, subject to separate permissions and capacity planning. Keep another independent or off-site recovery copy for critical data; a single NAS failure must not erase both primary data and every backup. See the [segmentation decision](../decisions/0003-segmented-services-and-remote-access.md) for the optional storage VLAN and restricted cross-VLAN data/backup paths.

Direct NAS hosting is a candidate for Jellyfin, Nextcloud, and maintained production Bitwarden. Before selecting hardware/software, verify the shared NAS VLAN controls, private NAS management, workload-specific permissions, updates, MFA/application authentication, backup/export, and full restore. Test isolated app/VM networks or VLAN-tagged interfaces only if a distinct guest VLAN becomes necessary. A managed-switch access port places the entire NAS on one VLAN; the router VM cannot separate applications sharing that host's network stack. The final per-service placement and whether direct NAS hosting is appropriate remain `TODO`. The Acer remains a separate intended local-AI compute node; NAS storage for its model/data files is optional after access and performance tests.
